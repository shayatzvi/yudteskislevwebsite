Why we are farbrenging

In 1798, Rabbi Schneur Zalman of Liadi, the Alter Rebbe and founder of Chabad, was arrested on false charges and imprisoned in the Peter and Paul Fortress in St. Petersburg.

On 19 Kislev he was set free. Chassidim celebrate it as a victory for the teachings of Chassidus, with farbrengens, thanks and good resolutions.

פָּדָה בְשָׁלוֹם נַפְשִׁי

"He redeemed my soul in peace" (Tehillim 55:19). The Alter Rebbe was reciting these words when he learned he was free.

About our farbrengen

What is a farbrengen?

A Chassidic gathering with food on the table, l'chaim in hand and honest words between friends.

What makes 19 Kislev special?

It is the day of the Alter Rebbe's liberation, known as the Rosh Hashanah of Chassidus. It is also the yahrzeit of the Maggid of Mezritch.

Fleishiks, Lubavitch shechita.
